Sandbox_BMoG: a region of the BloodMoon Pack grid
This region comes from the sandbox of the BloodMoon Pack OpenSim grid. Thanks to Vlad, its owner.
It is shown here to demonstrate OpenSim 0.9.3 PBR materials and their new lighting: walk around (P key) and try the "Metallic reflections" setting in Settings.
In the middle of the region, a circle is a hop zone: walk onto it (or click it, or press E) to open a hop:// link to the same region in the BloodMoon Pack grid. You need an OpenSim viewer registered for hop://; the browser asks for confirmation.
Here you can only look: no object can be collected in this web version.
